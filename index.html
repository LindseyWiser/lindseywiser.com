About Me

I’m an astronomer and postdoctoral researcher studying the atmospheres of planets beyond our Solar System. My research combines space telescope observations with atmospheric modeling to better understand the formation, evolution, and diversity of distant worlds. I’m particularly interested in how new telescopes and advances in modeling can reveal the physical processes shaping exoplanets, placing our Solar Sytem in a cosmic context. I earned a B.S. in Engineering Mechanics and Earth and Planetary Science from Johns Hopkins University and a Ph.D. in Astrophysics from Arizona State University.

I’m also passionate about preserving the space environment and promoting the responsible use of space. I volunteer with the Space Generation Advisory Council as a Coordinator of the Space Generation Advocacy and Policy Platform, and serve on volunteer policy committees with the American Astronomical Society.

Outside of science, I love books, the outdoors, my cat, and collecting new hobbies. I’m happiest when I’m learning something new.
